Selected Output

Key Scientific Publications

Pioneering articles introducing and applying the GNXAS method.

Ab initio multiple-scattering approach to EXAFS

Fundamental theoretical foundation paper introducing curved-wave MS expansion, 3-body atomic correlation functions $g_3(r, \theta)$, and Hedin-Lundqvist potentials in GNXAS.

A. Filipponi, A. Di Cicco, and C.R. Natoli • Phys. Rev. B 52, 15122

Liquid-liquid phase transition in undercooled elements

Direct experimental observation of structural liquid-liquid transformations under extreme laser-heated Diamond Anvil Cell (DAC) pressure conditions.

A. Di Cicco et al. • J. Phys.: Condens. Matter 21, 285106

Asymmetric pair distribution functions in liquid metals

Exact structural refinement of thermal disorder and asymmetric non-Gaussian pair distributions in liquid Cu, Ag, Au, and Lead using GNXAS.

A. Di Cicco and A. Filipponi • Phys. Rev. B 51, 12598

Reverse Monte Carlo structural modeling (RMC-GNXAS)

Combining 3D RMC structural simulation boxes with GNXAS curved-wave EXAFS multiple-scattering signals in disordered glasses.

A. Di Cicco, A. Trapananti et al. • Phys. Rev. B 82, 064202

X-ray absorption spectroscopy under extreme conditions

Comprehensive review of high-pressure EXAFS techniques inside Diamond Anvil Cells (DAC) and Paris-Edinburgh presses at ESRF and Elettra beamlines.

A. Di Cicco & A. Trapananti • J. Synchrotron Rad. 23, 1145

Local structure of battery materials and chalcogenides

Investigating short-range order, ion solvation under pressure, and phase-change optical recording materials ($GeSe_2$, $GeSbTe$).

S.J. Rezvani, M. Minicucci et al. • ACS Applied Materials
