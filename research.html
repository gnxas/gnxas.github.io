XAS LAB • Experimental & Theoretical Physics

Probing Matter Under Extreme Conditions & Disordered States

The X-ray Absorption Spectroscopy Laboratory (XAS LAB) at Unicam investigates local atomic structure, electronic transitions, and phase transformations in liquids, glasses, nanomaterials, and matter subjected to extreme pressures and temperatures.

Explore Core Topics Synchrotron Facilities
Scientific Pillars

Core Research Areas

Our research combines cutting-edge synchrotron X-ray absorption (XAS/EXAFS/XANES) and diffraction (XRD) techniques with advanced GNXAS multi-body structural refinements.

🧪

Liquids & Undercooled Melts

In-depth structural characterization of liquid metals, semiconductors, and undercooled melts. Determining 3D local coordination and short-range ordering via $g_2(r)$ and $g_3(r, \theta)$ functions.

Liquid Metals • Glasses • g₃(r,θ)
⚡

Extreme High-Pressure & High-Temperature

Pioneering EXAFS experiments inside Diamond Anvil Cells (DAC) and Paris-Edinburgh presses reaching several GPa and thousands of Kelvin to uncover novel liquid-liquid phase transitions.

DAC • Paris-Edinburgh Press
🔋

Nanomaterials & Energy Physics

Probing size-dependent lattice distortions, surface relaxation, ion hydration under pressure, battery materials, and phase-change chalcogenides ($GeSe_2$, $GeSbTe$).

Nanoparticles • Batteries • Ion Hydration
International Infrastructure

Synchrotron Facility Collaborations

Our experimental research relies on intense beamlines at major European synchrotron radiation laboratories.

European Synchrotron (ESRF)

High-brilliance X-ray absorption spectroscopy and energy-dispersive diffraction experiments on beamlines BM23, ID24, and ID27 for ultra-high pressure DAC studies.

Grenoble, France • BM23 / ID24 / ID27

Elettra Sincrotrone Trieste

Longstanding collaboration on XAFS and XPRESS beamlines for X-ray absorption spectroscopy, ambient and high-pressure diffraction, and energy materials characterization.

Trieste, Italy • XAFS / XPRESS

PETRA III at DESY

Extreme conditions beamline (P02.2) and X-ray absorption spectroscopy beamlines for fast time-resolved measurements and high-resolution structural studies.

Hamburg, Germany • P02.2 Beamline

Diamond Light Source & ALBA

Collaborative research experiments at Diamond Light Source (B18 EXAFS beamline) and ALBA Synchrotron (CLAESS beamline) for functional nanomaterials.

Didcot, UK & Barcelona, Spain
