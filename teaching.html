Degree Programs

Physics Courses at UNICAM

Core undergraduate and graduate courses offered by the Physics of Matter division.

📚

Physics of Matter (Fisica della Materia)

Fundamental principles of atomic physics, quantum mechanics of solids, crystal structures, lattice dynamics, and optical/electronic properties of matter.

Instructor: Prof. Andrea Di Cicco • Master Degree
🔬

X-ray Absorption Spectroscopy & Synchrotron Physics

Advanced experimental methods in synchrotron radiation, EXAFS/XANES theory, multiple-scattering calculations, and data fitting using GNXAS.

Instructor: Prof. Angela Trapananti • Graduate Course
🧪

Condensed Matter Physics & Extreme Conditions

Physics of liquids, glasses, high-pressure Diamond Anvil Cell techniques, phase transitions under high P-T, and disordered systems.

Instructors: Prof. A. Di Cicco & Team • PhD Level
Student Opportunities

Master & PhD Thesis Topics

Students interested in experimental synchrotron physics or theoretical code development are encouraged to join the group.

Synchrotron Radiation Experiments

Participation in on-site beamline data collection campaigns at Elettra (Trieste) or ESRF (Grenoble) for liquid metals and DAC studies.

Computational Physics & RMC Modeling

Developing 3D structural models of amorphous chalcogenides and battery nanomaterials combining Reverse Monte Carlo with GNXAS.

High-Pressure Data Analysis

Refining energy-dispersive X-ray diffraction (EDXRD) and XAS data taken inside Paris-Edinburgh and Diamond Anvil Cells.
