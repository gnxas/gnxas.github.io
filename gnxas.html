The GNXAS Method & Suite

Advanced ab initio multiple-scattering (MS) calculations and non-linear structural refinement directly fit against raw X-ray absorption spectra (EXAFS & XANES).

Core Principles of GNXAS

Beyond single-scattering Fourier transforms: full multi-body expansion.

N-Body Distribution Functions

Direct determination of 2-atom ($g_2$), 3-atom ($g_3$), and 4-atom ($g_4$) structural correlation functions without ad-hoc assumptions.

γ = γ⁽²⁾ + γ⁽³⁾ + γ⁽⁴⁾

Hedin-Lundqvist Potentials

Self-consistent muffin-tin atomic potential construction incorporating complex Hedin-Lundqvist exchange-correlation potentials.
